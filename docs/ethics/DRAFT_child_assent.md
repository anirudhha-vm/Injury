## Hello!
We are researchers who want to learn how growing and moving are linked to sports injuries, so
that young athletes can be looked after better.

## What would you do?
- Every couple of months we measure your height and weight and film you doing some jumps and
  squats.
- Every week you answer a few quick questions about any pain and how much you trained.

## Things to know
- You don't have to take part. It's your choice, even if your parent said yes.
- You can stop at any time and nobody will be upset. It won't change anything about your team.
- Your videos are kept private on one computer and are not shared or put on the internet.
- If anything hurts during the tasks, tell us and we will stop.
- You can ask us questions at any time.
