We are researchers at TODO(human: institution) working on methods to estimate biological
maturity (age at peak height velocity) from sparse anthropometric measurements in youth
athletes. Our model is a Bayesian growth-curve "growth twin" that updates each athlete's
estimate as new measurements arrive and reports its uncertainty. We have developed and
validated it on the Berkeley Child Guidance Study and would like to test it externally in
youth soccer players.

The data availability statement of Malina et al. (2021), "Observed and predicted ages at peak
height velocity in soccer players", PLoS ONE 16(7):e0254659, indicates that the data are
available on request from CIDAF under a confidential disclosure agreement. We would be grateful
for access to the de-identified longitudinal records used there:

- player code (no names), date of birth (month/year is sufficient), measurement dates;
- height, sitting height and body mass at each annual observation;
- the SITAR-derived age at peak height velocity, if it can be shared.

The data would be used only for this validation study, stored securely without identifiers,
not shared further, and acknowledged as you request; we are happy to sign the disclosure
agreement and to offer co-authorship to the data custodians if appropriate.
