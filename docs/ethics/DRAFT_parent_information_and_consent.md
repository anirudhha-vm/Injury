## What is this study?
We are studying how growth and movement relate to injuries in young athletes, to help coaches
and physiotherapists support young athletes better in future. This is research. It is **not** a
medical test and will not diagnose your child.

## What will my child do?
- Every 6–8 weeks: height, sitting height and weight measured; a short set of jumping and
  squatting tasks filmed by two phones.
- Every week: a short questionnaire about pain or injury and how much they trained.
- If your child has an injury, the academy physiotherapist will record the diagnosis as usual.
- The study lasts about one season (9–12 months).

## What information is collected and how is it protected?
- Your child is given a random code. Names are kept separately from study data.
- **Videos stay on a secured local computer at the research institution. They are never
  uploaded to the internet or cloud services and never shared.** Only body-position points
  computed from the video are kept for analysis.
- Date of birth is stored as month and year only.

## Risks and benefits
The tasks are similar to normal training. There is no direct benefit to your child; study
predictions are not used to make decisions about your child during the study.

Taking part is voluntary. You or your child can withdraw at any time without giving a reason and
