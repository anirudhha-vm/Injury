# M11 plan — Path A: a growth twin validated on real data (2026-10-23)

## Goal
Turn the paper into a methods paper on single-season maturity estimation that rests on real data
only, and earn the "digital twin" claim: a persistent per-child state, updated as each
measurement arrives, that forecasts the next measurement with calibrated uncertainty.

## Design
- **Reference-cohort training.** The shared growth curve is learned from the full records
  (8-18 y) of training children. Each training child's Moore prior is computed from a random
  one-season window, as it would be at deployment, so the learned spread of the prior reflects
  real prior error.
- **Distance-dependent timing prior (coverage fix).** Prior SD of timing
  `sigma_beta * (1 + kappa * |Moore maturity offset|)`, `kappa` learned (half-normal prior).
- **Sequential twin evaluation, held-out children.** Five folds grouped by child, per sex. Each
  held-out child enters at a random measurement age (8-15 y); the twin is updated after each new
  visit (up to six, i.e. three years) against the fold's shared draws. At every step:
  age at PHV (error, bias, interval coverage, width) and a six-month-ahead stature forecast
  (error, predictive-interval coverage); comparators Moore and Mirwald (boys) from the same
  visits, and velocity extrapolation for the forecast.
- Reported by number of visits and by years from PHV.

## Files
`src/youthtwin/growth/sitar_bayes.py` (distance-dependent prior, prior from a separate frame),
`src/youthtwin/growth/growth_state.py` (store the slope), `src/youthtwin/external/sequential.py`,
`scripts/real_twin_sequential.py`, `configs/external/sequential.yaml`, tests for each.

## Acceptance
- With `kappa = 0` the model and its likelihood are unchanged (test).
- Sequential evaluation never uses a held-out child's data in training, nor visits after the
  current step in its twin (tests).
- Runs end to end on Berkeley; outputs `synthetic=False` with provenance.

A second real longitudinal dataset (Indian cohort best; else PBMAS, Zurich, Fels or ALSPAC by
application); Mirwald girls' coefficient check; journal choice; domain co-author; affiliations.
