# M4 plan — validation module (Paper A)

## Goal
Agreement statistics between smartphone markerless kinematics and marker-based motion capture,
per variable, task and event, stratified by maturity group and sex — implemented and checked
against known answers before any lab data exist.

## Files
- `src/youthtwin/validation/agreement.py`
  - bias, RMSE, mean absolute error;
  - Bland–Altman: bias, SD of differences, 95% limits of agreement with confidence intervals
    (Bland & Altman 1999), proportional bias (slope of difference on mean);
  - ICC(2,1) and ICC(3,1), single measurement, with F-based confidence intervals (Shrout &
    Fleiss 1979; McGraw & Wong 1996);
  - `agreement_table`: one row per variable × task × event × stratum, with the pre-specified
    acceptance check from the config.
- `src/youthtwin/validation/mocap_io.py` — read marker trajectories from TRC (plain text) and,
  if the optional reader is installed, C3D; time alignment of a mocap angle curve to a
  markerless one by cross-correlation; resampling onto a common clock.
- `src/youthtwin/validation/plots.py` — Bland–Altman grid, RMSE by maturity stratum, curve
  overlays; watermarked when synthetic.
- `configs/validation/agreement.yaml` — limits-of-agreement level, acceptance threshold
  (provisional, from the pre-registration), maturity bands.
- `scripts/validate_markerless.py` — paired feature tables → agreement table and figures.

## Tests
- `test_agreement.py`
  - recovery on synthetic paired data with known bias and noise (bias, SD, limits, RMSE);
  - Bland–Altman and ICC equal to hand-worked values on a small published-style example;
  - ICC(2,1) and ICC(3,1) equal `pingouin.intraclass_corr` to numerical precision;
  - proportional bias detected when built in, absent when not;
  - stratified table structure and acceptance flags.
- `test_mocap_io.py` — TRC round trip; time alignment recovers a known lag; missing optional
  C3D reader gives an actionable error.

## Acceptance criteria (brief §7)
- Agreement metrics verified on synthetic paired data with known bias and noise.
- Bland–Altman and ICC match a reference implementation.
- `make test lint` green.
