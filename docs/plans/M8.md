# M8 plan — research dashboard and decision-support text

## Goal
A local, browser-based view of one athlete's digital twin for the research team, and a small,
auditable rule set that turns model outputs into decision-support prompts — every prompt framed
as "consider", tied to a cited evidence entry, and never a diagnosis or instruction (N9).

## Files
- `src/youthtwin/recommend/evidence_library.yaml` — evidence entries (statement, citation,
  caveat), each marked as needing verification against the original paper.
- `src/youthtwin/recommend/rules.py` — rules from model outputs to prompts. No rule produces an
  "elevated risk" prompt from maturity alone (v2 had such a rule).
- `src/youthtwin/dashboard/bundle.py` — builds and loads the dashboard data bundle: per athlete,
  measurements, fitted growth curve with band, APHV interval, velocity timeline, out-of-fold
  4-week risk per injury type with an uncertainty band from growth-posterior draws, SHAP
  contributions, screening keypoints for replay, and prompts.
- `src/youthtwin/dashboard/figures.py` — the plots (growth curve, velocity, risk timeline,
  contributions, skeleton frame), watermarked when synthetic.
- `apps/twin_dashboard/app.py` — Streamlit UI over a bundle directory. Local only; no
  authentication, deployment or monitoring (out of scope for the paper).
- `scripts/build_dashboard.py`; `make dashboard`.
- `configs/dashboard.yaml` — thresholds for prompts, number of draws, grid sizes.

## Tests
- Every prompt contains "consider", cites at least one evidence entry that exists, and contains
  no diagnostic or prescriptive wording.
- Maturity alone never triggers an elevated-risk prompt.
- Bundle tables are complete and flagged synthetic; risk shown for an athlete is out-of-fold.
- The app runs headlessly on a synthetic bundle without exceptions (Streamlit's test runner).

## Acceptance criteria (brief §7)
- Dashboard runs on the synthetic cohort.
- All text is decision-support framed.

## Notes
- Skeleton replay is drawn from keypoints; no video is involved or referenced.
- The risk numbers shown come from a synthetic cohort where models are near chance (see
  `M7_done.md`); the dashboard demonstrates presentation, not predictive value, and says so on
  screen.
